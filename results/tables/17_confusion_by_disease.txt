17_confusion_by_disease.py -- by-disease confusion, nine sources, six conditions
==============================================================================

Source construction reused verbatim from 13c_evaluate_all_sources.py's load_merged_df (13c:180-199) via importlib -- reads 08b_slice_scores.csv and 13b_new_slice_scores.csv, the same two files this script itself reads no other numbers from.
Also reused: SOURCES_ORDER (13c:134-135), TRUTH_SOURCES (13c:136), CONDITIONS_ALL (13c:139-140), CLASSES (13c:125, from 08c:59-61).
NOT reused: outcome_column/correct_for/risk_definition_for/risk_score_for (13c:213-229, 08c:147-162) -- none of this script's tables need a "correct" boolean.
Written fresh, no equivalent function exists in 13c to import: accepted_mask(), mirroring the accepted/deferred dispatch inlined in 13c's build_t07 (13c:358-364), build_t09 (13c:426-431) and build_t12 (13c:496-502).

'Accepted' definition, used throughout: condition 1 = every row in the source (nothing deferred); conditions 2/3/4/5 = ~defer_condition{N}; condition B2 = ~defer_B2.
